Growth Drivers — Why Companies MUST Digitize NOW

  • New Tax Laws (GST, Aadhaar): Indian government requires businesses to track sales, inventory, and employee data digitally. Companies must upgrade old systems or face penalties. This forces even small businesses to modernize.

  • Startups Are Scaling Fast: Backed by VC funding, startups (companies 2-5 years old with ₹50Cr+ revenue) need software urgently. They have money to spend and need delivery quickly. This is OUR target market.

Revenue & EBITDA Path (24 Months)

📊 What These Numbers Mean (in Simple Terms):
  • Revenue: Total money coming IN from clients (projects + support + academy + FDE engineers). This is the TOP line.
  • Gross Margin: After paying delivery team, how much money is left (as %). Example: ₹100 revenue - ₹55 delivery cost = ₹45 profit margin = 45% margin.
  • EBITDA: Money LEFT after paying all costs (team, rent, marketing, everything). If positive = company is profitable. If negative = company is spending more than earning.
  • Recurring Revenue: Money that comes EVERY MONTH automatically (like Netflix monthly subscription). More recurring = more stable business.
