#### **Slide 10: Revenue & EBITDA Path (24 Months)**

**What These Numbers Mean:**
- **Revenue:** Total money coming IN from clients (projects + support + academy + FDE). TOP line.
- **Gross Margin:** After paying delivery team, how much money is left (%). Example: ₹100 revenue - ₹55 delivery cost = 45% margin.
- **EBITDA:** Money LEFT after all costs (team, rent, marketing, everything). If positive = profitable.
- **Recurring Revenue:** Money coming EVERY MONTH automatically (like Netflix). More recurring = more stable.
