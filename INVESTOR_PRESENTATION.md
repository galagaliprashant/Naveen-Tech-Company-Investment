## SLIDE 3: OUR SOLUTION

### Three-Tier Service Offering

#### **Phase 1: Digital Transformation & Growth Services (Years 1–2)**

**Core Services Package:**
- **Web & Mobile Development** — websites, web apps, iOS/Android custom software
- **Workflow Digitisation** — CRM/ERP integrations, automation, dashboards, analytics
- **AI-Enabled Solutions** — document processing, customer insights, predictive workflows
- **Digital Growth Services** — performance marketing, SEO, content, campaign analytics
- **Ongoing Support** — 24/7 managed support, bug fixes, feature improvements

**Engagement Model:**
- Fixed-scope, outcome-led contracts (3–6 months)
- Clear acceptance criteria, timelines, exclusions, change control
- Dedicated account manager + technical lead
- Pricing: ₹25–60 lakhs per project; 12–18 month support contracts @ ₹3–8 lakhs/year

**Target Clients:**
- Founders & COOs (₹50Cr–500Cr ARR companies)
- Growth-stage enterprises looking to modernise
- Regional players expanding into new markets

---

#### **Phase 2: FDE Academy (Year 2+ Pilot)**

**What We Offer:**
- 8–12 week, hands-on full-stack engineering bootcamp
- Focus: cloud, AI-assisted development, client communication, real project delivery
- Practical curriculum (60% hands-on, 40% theory)
- Taught by company engineers + industry mentors

**Why It Works:**
- Creates a talent pipeline for Phase 3 (Managed FDE)
- Generates academy revenue (₹3–5L per cohort of 15–20 engineers)
- Establishes brand as a trusted training partner
- **Strict start conditions:** only after services business is stable, curriculum approved, paid pilot cohort recruited

---

#### **Phase 3: Managed Forward Deployed Engineers (Year 2+)**

**What We Sell:**
- Dedicated, accountable engineers working *with* your team, *managed by us*
- Service levels: 99.5% uptime, same-day escalation, replacement coverage
- Monthly contract model: ₹8–15L/month per engineer or managed squad
- Performance metrics: delivery velocity, quality standards, response time

**Why Investors Love This:**
- 70%+ gross margins (once volume established)
- Recurring revenue (contracts auto-renew)
- Clients stick longer (engineers are *embedded*, not transactional)
- Scalable: one manager supervises 8–12 FDEs

## APPENDIX C: GLOSSARY & TERMS

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **FDE** | Forward Deployed Engineer — a company employee working embedded with a client, managed by the company |
| **ARR** | Annual Recurring Revenue — revenue that renews every year (support contracts, subscriptions) |
| **EBITDA** | Earnings Before Interest, Tax, Depreciation, Amortization — operating profit (cash generation) |
| **Gross Margin** | Revenue minus direct costs (delivery team, infra). Higher margins = more profitable scaling. |
| **Burn Rate** | Monthly cash spend (salaries, overhead). Positive when revenue > burn. |
| **TAM** | Total Addressable Market — the entire market opportunity if we captured 100% |
| **CAC** | Customer Acquisition Cost — how much we spend to land one customer |
| **Cohort** | A batch of academy students who enroll and graduate together |
| **Tranche** | A scheduled disbursement of investment capital based on milestones |
